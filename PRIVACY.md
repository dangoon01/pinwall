# Privacy Policy — MUKSAN PinWall

_Last updated: 2026-11-19_

MUKSAN PinWall is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by its developer, on the developer's own computer, with the developer's own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## What data the app accesses

With the account owner's authorization through Pinterest OAuth, the app reads:

- Basic account information (`user_accounts:read`)
- The account owner's own boards (`boards:read`)
- The account owner's own Pins, including image URLs and creation dates (`pins:read`)

The app is read-only. It does not create, edit or delete Pins or boards, and it does not access data belonging to other Pinterest users.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to display the account owner's Pins as an image wall, to filter them by date, board and topic, and to keep a local backup of saved images for personal reference.

## Storage and sharing

- All data, including OAuth tokens, is stored locally on the developer's own computer.
- No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

## Retention and deletion

Locally stored data can be deleted at any time by removing the app's data folder. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account's security settings, after which the app can no longer read any data.

## Contact
